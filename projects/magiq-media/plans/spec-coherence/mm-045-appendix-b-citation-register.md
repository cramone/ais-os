## ⚠ Namespace collision — read before working this register

`CR-`, `AM-`, `MI-` and `M-` in the register are off-repo **finding** ids from a prior review. `CR-`, `MI-`,
`MP-` and `RT-` are also **MM-044's own finding prefixes** (see its front-matter `exception:` line). Until the
register is worked, `CR-16` in a spec file and `CR-16` in MM-044 are different things wearing the same name.

Two consequences:

1. **Strip, do not renumber.** Renumbering MM-044's findings to dodge the clash would break citations already
   circulated on 2026-09-15 for no gain; the clash disappears when the spec-side ids are removed.
2. **`DS-` is mixed and must be handled by token, not by family.** `DS-1`–`DS-5` are in-tree scenario ids and
   stay. `DS-7` and `DS-9` are off-repo finding ids and go. A family-wide sweep here destroys real content.
